# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users

People listening to public-domain books, imported PDFs, and manga on iPhone. They browse a catalog stored on the device, resume a chapter, download for offline, and turn on narration when a title has no human recording. Inferred from the shipping app and its onboarding copy.

## Product Purpose

AudioBy keeps a personal listening library: search the public-domain catalog, play chapter audio, save a place, import a PDF, and read manga. The catalog stays free. Premium adds studio voices, extra offline titles, and extra reader scenes.

## Positioning

One library for streamed public-domain audio, on-device narration of books that were never recorded, and imported files, with the place saved when the listener comes back.

## Operating Context

iPhone, including one-handed listening, the lock screen, and a large-type driving layout. Light and dark appearance are both first-class. A separate marketing site exists beside this app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed: LibriVox and Internet Archive catalog, Gutenberg text, PDF import, manga shelves, bookmarks, sleep timer, car mode, downloads, streaks, and sign-in. Destructive actions stay red. Do not invent prices, testimonials, or catalog counts beyond what the app already shows.

## Brand Commitments

Name: AudioBy. Binding palette, in order: #fffcf2 paper, #ccc5b9 stone, #403d39 charcoal, #252422 ink, #eb5e28 ember. Ember is the only accent. Ink sits on ember. The listening mark is an ember band along the bottom of a cover.

## Evidence on Hand

Onboarding copy, catalog models, and the native screens under AudioBy/UI. No customer quotes or press. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- The listener should find play, progress, and the current title before anything else.
- Paper and ink carry reading; ember marks only what can be acted on or is already in progress.
- Offline, imported, and streamed titles stay in one library.
- Light and dark use the same five colors, swapped as ground and ink.
